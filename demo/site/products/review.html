Aurora X1 vs Nimbus Pro after a month

Call quality: the Aurora X1 is clearly better in windy streets; the Nimbus Pro sounds muffled outdoors.

Comfort: Aurora X1 can be worn all day. Nimbus Pro clamps harder.

Sound: Nimbus Pro wins for music with more detail; Aurora X1 has heavier bass.

Value: Nimbus Pro costs 50 USD less and includes a cable for wired listening.

Verdict: frequent travellers should pick Aurora X1; music lovers on a budget, Nimbus Pro.
